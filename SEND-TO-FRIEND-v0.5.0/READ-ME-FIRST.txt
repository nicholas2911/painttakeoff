PAINTTAKEOFF — TEST PACKAGE (v0.3.1)
====================================

What's in here:
  1. PaintTakeoff-Setup-0.3.1.exe  ← the app installer (double-click it)
  2. PaintTakeoff-Guide.pdf        ← 5-minute how-to guide

INSTALLING (one time only):
  Double-click the Setup file. Windows may show a blue
  "Windows protected your PC" popup — click "More info" → "Run anyway".
  It installs itself in about 30 seconds and puts a PaintTakeoff icon
  on your desktop. From then on, open it from there.

UPDATES COME TO YOU NOW — no more new files from us:
  When we improve the app, a flashing "New update" button appears at
  the top of the app. Click it → it downloads → click "Restart to
  update". That's the whole thing. You never need to reinstall.

WHAT TO TRY (about 10 minutes):
  1. Open one of your own plan PDFs (drag it onto the window, or use
     the Open Plan button).
  2. Pick a floor plan page, click "Set Scale", click the two ends of a
     wall whose length is written on the plan, type the number in.
     (On a metric plan, the "Common scales…" dropdown has 1:75 / 1:50 /
     1:100 etc., or just type the millimetres as printed. If the badge
     stays amber, click it — it runs the double-check.)
  3. Click "Measure" and measure a few walls: click to start, keep
     clicking to add segments, double-click to finish. Check the numbers
     against walls you know. Open "Measurements" to see the page total.
  4. Click "Quick Area", then click inside a room — it fills the room and
     gives a rough wall square footage (set the ceiling height). Tweak
     the numbers, keep the room.

  - How long would this save/take you vs. how you do it now?
